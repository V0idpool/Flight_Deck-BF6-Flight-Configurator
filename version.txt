1.1.0.0
Flight Deck v1.1.0.0 Patch notes

- Fixed issue with binding LB/RB and L1/R1, before it was flipped and firing was on LB/L1, and zoom/countermeasures/other actions were on RB/R1 even though on the visual controller map it displayed properly, ingame it was flipped/reversed
- updated control schemes so BuildBf4Veteran(), BuildBf3Classic(), and BuildBf2042Alternate(), etc. accurately display LB / L1 = Fire Weapon and RB / R1 = Countermeasures / Zoom / Spot to match Battlefield 6's Buttons = 1 preset
- fixed visual controller control to properly move the indicator line when the dpad and control sticks flip on PS controller
- Updated the Visual Controller control and view to allow custom controller bindings. You can now click a binding on the controller visualizer, and bind it to a controller button of your choosing. This makes customization of flight controls even easier, and allows further tuning to share your exported Flight Deck profiles.
- revamped import/export custom profile system. You can now name your profile(s), and add a description that will be shown in the Flight Control Scheme & Stick Tuning section, load multiple profiles, and optionally delete profiles.
- fixed profile save dropdown box, it no longer clips into the background of the UI
- Persistent settings via saving to settings json file, your custom settings will now load on app start.
- Added ExceptionHandler logging, errors and crashes will be saved to FD_Logs.txt in the base Flight Deck directory.
